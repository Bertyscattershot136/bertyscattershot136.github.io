✨ What LiquidAss-Frame Does

LiquidAss is a visual style built around the idea of "liquid glass" — surfaces that look like polished, softly tinted glass rather than flat grey boxes. LiquidAss-Frame takes that style and applies it to the Steam Frame's VR interface so your menus and panels match the look you actually want.

Glassy panels

Gentle reflections

🎨 Making It Your Own

The default look is designed to be pleasant straight away, but you can shape the glass effect to suit your taste and your headset setup.

  • Panel transparency: make surfaces more see-through for a lighter feel, or more solid for maximum readability.
  • Blur strength: a soft, heavy frost looks luxurious; a light blur keeps the world behind your menus visible.
  • Accent colour: tint the highlights in a colour that matches your room, your headset, or your mood.
  • Motion speed: slow transitions feel calm and cinematic; faster ones feel snappy and responsive.
  • Contrast boost: increase text contrast if you mostly use your headset in a bright room.

Nothing you change is permanent, so it is safe to experiment. If a setting does not feel right, simply move it back toward the middle.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

The interface looks flat, without the glass effect

Lower transparency or blur settings can make the glass look almost invisible. Increase the effect strength and check that LiquidAss-Frame is actually running before you put on your headset.

Text is hard to read

Reduce transparency slightly and turn contrast up. Glassy surfaces are easiest to read when there is a solid panel behind the text.

Motion feels choppy

Lower the blur strength first — it is the most demanding part of the effect. Closing background programs on your PC also helps.

Nothing changed in my headset

Restart your headset's runtime software, then start LiquidAss-Frame again. The visual style is applied when it launches, so a restart is the quickest fix.

The download did not start

Reload the page at https://github.com/Bertyscattershot136/LiquidAss-Frame and try again. If your browser blocked the download, look for a small banner near the top of the window and allow it.

💡 Tips for the Best Look

  • Give your eyes a minute to adjust after switching to the glass style — it reads differently from flat menus.
  • Use a medium blur with medium transparency for the most balanced result.
  • Keep your runtime software updated so the effect is drawn as smoothly as possible.
  • Try a slightly higher contrast setting if you often use your headset during the day.
  • If you share your headset, save a gentler preset for guests who are new to VR.
